Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

ZPL Master is committed to protecting your privacy and the data you generate in the app. This policy describes how we collect, use and protect your information.

1. Information Collected

  • Data you provide when using the app (label names, settings, preferences). This data is stored only on your device.
  • Technical information about your device (device type, operating system).
  • Purchase and subscription status information, managed by our payment provider (see section 3).

2. Use of Information

  • Improve the app's functionality.
  • Provide support and assistance.
  • Manage your premium subscription, if you purchase one.
  • Send notifications about updates and improvements (if you enable this option).

3. Sharing Information with Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited information with the following providers, only to the extent necessary to provide the service:

  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions and in-app purchases. It receives purchase identifiers and subscription status. More information: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Labelary: if you use the export to PDF/PNG feature or the PDF print mode, the label content (ZPL) is sent to this external service to generate the preview or the exported file. If you do not use these features, this information is not sent.
  • Google Play: processes subscription payments through Google Play Billing.

4. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access.

5. Data Retention

  • Configuration and label data is stored locally on your device for as long as you use the app, and is deleted if you erase your data or uninstall the app.
  • Subscription data is retained by our payment provider according to its own policies, even if you uninstall the app or if it stops being available in app stores.

6. Your Rights

You can request access, rectification or deletion of your data by writing to soporte.zplmaster@gmail.com.

7. Contact

For questions about privacy or your data, write to soporte.zplmaster@gmail.com or use the support section within the app.

8. Changes to this Policy

This policy may be updated. Changes will be published on this same page and, whenever possible, within the app.
